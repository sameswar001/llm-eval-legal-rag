COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of January 1, 2026, between Fjordline Properties AB ("Landlord") and Ember & Co ("Tenant"), for the premises located at Kungsgatan 22, Stockholm, Sweden ("Premises").

1. Term. The initial term of this Lease is five (5) years, commencing January 1, 2026 and ending December 31, 2030 ("Initial Term").

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly rent of SEK 45,000, due on the first day of each calendar month. Rent shall increase annually on each anniversary of the commencement date by the change in the Swedish Consumer Price Index, capped at 4% per year.

3. Renewal Option. Tenant shall have the option to renew this Lease for up to two (2) additional periods of three (3) years each, provided Tenant gives Landlord written notice of intent to renew at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to two (2) months' rent upon signing, refundable within thirty (30) days after the end of the Lease, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

5. Maintenance. Landlord shall be responsible for maintaining the structural elements of the building, including the roof, exterior walls, and foundation. Tenant shall be responsible for maintaining the interior of the Premises, including fixtures, flooring, and interior walls, in good condition.

6. Use of Premises. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office and retail purposes and shall not use the Premises for any unlawful purpose.

7. Termination for Default. If Tenant fails to pay rent within fifteen (15) days of the due date, Landlord may issue a notice of default. If the default is not cured within fifteen (15) days of such notice, Landlord may terminate this Lease and pursue any remedies available under Swedish law.

8. Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of Sweden.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Lease as of the date first written above.
